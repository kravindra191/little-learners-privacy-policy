Please read this with a parent or guardian. The words are plain on purpose.

1. Introduction

Little Learners (Kids Learn) is an app for young children. Children learn English letters, the Hindi alphabet, and numbers by studying flashcards, tracing letters and numbers, and playing games.

Er Ravindra made this app. This policy explains what the app does with information.

2. Information we collect

This app collects none. It does not ask for or collect a name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or device identifiers.

It does not collect the Android advertising ID (AAID) or any other device identifier. There is no account, so it does not collect account information. It does not use location.

  • Personal and sensitive user data accessed: none.
  • Personal and sensitive user data collected: none.
  • Personal and sensitive user data used: none.
  • Personal and sensitive user data shared: none.
  • Parties data is shared with: none.

3. Data stored on the device

The only information saved is learning progress: the stars and badges a child earns. They are stored in the app's private storage on this device. Other apps cannot read that storage.

Er Ravindra does not receive the stars or badges, and they are not sent to a server. Android backup is turned off for this app, and its backup rules exclude its files. Progress is not uploaded and is not copied to another device by Android backup or device transfer.

The sound on or off choice is kept only while the app is in use. It is not saved as an account and it is not transmitted.

Uninstalling Little Learners deletes the saved progress. A parent can also delete it without uninstalling: open Android Settings, tap Apps, tap Little Learners, tap Storage, then tap Clear data or Clear storage. There is no copy on a server, so this removes all of it.

4. Voice and text-to-speech

The app uses the device's built-in text-to-speech to read letters, words, and numbers aloud. Speech is created on the device by the Android text-to-speech engine already installed there.

The app does not record the microphone, does not listen to the child, and does not send voice or text to a server.

5. Internet, ads, and third parties

The app works offline. It has no ads, no ad SDKs, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

It does not share information with third parties. There is nothing to share. There are no social features, no public profiles, and no chat. The app does not open websites.

The Privacy Policy link on the home screen opens this policy inside the app. A child cannot open it until a grown-up answers a short addition question. The Parents area uses the same question before it opens. Inside the app, the email address is text a parent can copy. It does not open another app or a website. A parent who has answered that question can open the device's text-to-speech settings to install a Hindi voice. That opens an Android settings screen, not a website, and it stays behind the same question. The app has no other link that leaves it.

6. Children's privacy

The target audience is children, including children under 13. Little Learners is designed for them. A parent or guardian should decide whether a child uses it.

The app is built to meet the Google Play Families Policy, including the rules for Designed for Families and Teacher Approved: no collection of personal information from children, no ads or ad software, no location, no advertising ID or other device identifiers, and no social features.

It is also built to meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR rules for children's data in the European Union (GDPR-K), and the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for children's data. Those rules limit collection, tracking, and targeted advertising directed at children. This app does not collect personal information from children, does not track them, and does not show ads.

7. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive Android permissions. It does not request Internet, location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos, storage, phone, SMS, or the advertising ID.

The installed app also carries one signature permission, com.erravindra767729.littlelearners.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. AndroidX adds this so the app's own internal parts can message each other. The user is not asked to grant it. It does not give access to personal data, and other apps cannot use it.

8. Data safety

This is the Data safety summary declared for Google Play Console:

  • No data collected
  • No data shared
  • Data is not encrypted in transit because none is transmitted
  • You can request data deletion

Stars and badges stay in private storage on the device. The developer cannot see them. No data is sent over the internet, so there is no transmission to encrypt. That is why data is not encrypted in transit.

Secure data handling: stars and badges stay in the app's private storage on the device. Other apps cannot read that storage. Android backup is off, so the progress is not uploaded. Nothing is sent over the internet. Data is not encrypted in transit because none is transmitted. The progress is not sold, rented, or shared.

9. How long data stays, and how to delete it

Personal data is not kept, because it is not collected. Stars and badges stay on the device until a parent clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app.

There is no account and no server copy. To request deletion, email kravindra191@gmail.com. Er Ravindra will reply and explain how to clear the on-device progress. A server copy cannot be deleted because none exists. You can request data deletion at any time. A parent can also delete the local progress by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in Android settings.

10. Changes to this policy

Users will be notified of changes by the updated policy shown inside the app and on the public privacy policy page. There is no account, so Er Ravindra cannot send an email or a push notice.

The updated policy will show a new effective date and a new last-updated date. The dates at the top of this page are the dates of the current version. Using the app after the new effective date means the updated policy applies.
